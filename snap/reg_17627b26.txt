Qualification evaluation fees Qualification evaluation fees Find out about the fees for an IQA All fees include GST (where applicable) and are subject to change. International Qualification Assessment (IQA) fees Note: add-ons can be added to your application up to 12 months after completion of your IQA. Standard IQA This is for the evaluation of one qualification NZ$445 Skill Shortage List IQA This is for the evaluation of one qualification and assessment under one occupation on an Immigration New Zealand Skill Shortage List NZ$610 Teaching IQA This is for the evaluation on your teaching qualification for the purpose of registering as a teacher with the Teaching Council, or for teacher salary assessment purposes This package includes evaluation of an additional 2 free Standard IQAs NZ$746 Teaching assessment add-on Apply for this add-on assessment for a Standard IQA you have already submitted or that was completed within 12 months NZ$301 (in addition to the previously paid IQA fee above) Skill Shortage List assessment add-on Apply for this add-on for a Standard IQA you have already submitted or that was completed within 12 months NZ$165 (in addition to the previously paid IQA fee above) Pre-1998 qualification fee – this fee applies if you completed your qualification before 1998 This is because the evaluation of older qualifications requires more time and resources NZ$275 (in addition to the IQA fee above) ← Scroll → Appeal fees The appeal fee is NZ$765. You can appeal the outcome of your evaluation within 20 working days from the date your recognition outcome statement was issued. Applications submitted before 30 July 2026 may apply for a review within 3 months of the published date on the recognition statement. You can contact us to discuss the outcome of your recognition statement before deciding to submit an appeal. Email the QRS team We will refund an appeal fee, less an administration fee of NZ$55, if a review results in a change to the recognition outcome based on the information and documents submitted in the original application. How we evaluate overseas qualifications Other fees If you require further administrative support: Email the QRS team You may also be required to pay services fees by third parties if we require further verification of your qualification. We will tell you if this is required. Fees for an Overseas Study Assessment (OSA) An OSA can let an New Zealander study an approved course overseas, with no extra interest on their student loan while they study. The fee for an OSA application is NZ$165. Cancellations and refunds Let us know in writing if you want to cancel your application. There is no refund if you choose to cancel your application.
