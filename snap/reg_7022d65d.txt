Graduate Explore Standards Proficient Explore Standards Highly Accomplished Explore Standards Lead Explore Standards Learn about the Standards About AITSL Supporting quality teaching and leadership in Australia Find out more about AITSL The Australian Institute for Teaching and School Leadership (AITSL) is a for-purpose organisation. We are dedicated to supporting teachers and leaders so that they can have the maximum impact on Australia’s children and young people. We develop and maintain resources for teachers and leaders, build frameworks and guidelines for educational institutions, and provide advice for the government. We’re most well-known for the Teacher Standards, and you can find hundreds of resources on our site based on this foundational document. It is our mission to promote excellence so that teachers and leaders can make a difference in Australian schools and early childhood settings.
