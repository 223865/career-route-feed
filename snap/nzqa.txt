Getting overseas qualifications recognised by NZQA Find out how and when to have an overseas qualification evaluated by NZQA for use in New Zealand. Make sure you have all the required documents before applying Only apply for an International Qualification Assessment (IQA) when you have all the required documents. Requirements for evidence and documents You must have all required documents ready before you apply. We start evaluating applications only when all required information and evidence have been provided. Why we evaluate overseas qualifications NZQA evaluates overseas tertiary and vocational qualifications to work out how they compare to New Zealand qualifications. Evaluation results can help New Zealand organisations better understand your overseas qualification for immigration, employment or further study. Check if you need an evaluation with the organisation you are applying to.
