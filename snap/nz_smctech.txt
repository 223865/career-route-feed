Live Resident visas to live in New Zealand Skilled residence pathways in New Zealand Getting New Zealand residence through the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) Getting New Zealand residence through the Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) Find out how the Skilled Migrant Category works, what the 3 pathways are, and if it is a good residence option for you. What the Skilled Migrant Category is The Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) is a residence visa for people who have skills New Zealand needs. You may be able to apply for an SMC Resident Visa if you have a skilled job or job offer in New Zealand and meet the requirements for 1 of the SMC pathways. The SMC Resident Visa lets you: live, work and study in New Zealand indefinitely include your partner and dependent children aged 24 or younger in your application, and apply for a Permanent Resident Visa later, if you meet the requirements. Is the Skilled Migrant Category right for you? The SMC may be an option if you have a skilled job or job offer in New Zealand and 1 of the following applies to you: you have a recognised qualification, like a bachelor's degree, master's degree or PhD you have eligible occupational registration, like registration as a plumber or membership as a chartered accountant you earn enough to claim points from your pay (income) you have at least 5 years of work experience, or you work in an eligible trade or technician role. The 3 SMC pathways There are 3 ways to qualify for the Skilled Migrant Category Resident Visa. Table 1: Overview of the 3 SMC pathways SMC pathway This may suit you if How it works Points-based pathway You have points from your qualification, occupational registration or pay You need 6 skilled resident points. You may be able to combine points from your qualification, occupational registration, or pay with work experience in New Zealand Skilled Work Experience pathway You have several years of skilled work experience You can qualify through work experience alone, if you have the required relevant work experience and skilled work experience in New Zealand Trades and Technician pathway You work in an eligible trades or technician role You can qualify through a mix of an eligible trades or technician job, a relevant qualification, and post-qualification work experience You only need to meet the requirements for 1 pathway. Requirements all SMC applicants must meet No matter which pathway you use, you must usually: have a job or job offer in New Zealand be paid a certain amount be in good health pass a character check (a police check), and be able to speak and understand English. If you include your partner or dependent children in your application, they may also need to meet health, character and English requirements. Points-based pathway The Points-based pathway is for people who can claim skilled resident points. You need 6 points to be eligible. You can claim points from 1 of these skill categories: qualifications pay from your job or job offer. Some people can get all 6 points from 1 category. For example, this may be possible if you have a PhD, a New Zealand master's degree that meets the requirements, an eligible occupational registration that requires significant training, or a very high income. If you cannot get all 6 points from 1 category, you may be able to add points from skilled work experience in New Zealand. Check your points You can use our points calculator to find out how many points you can claim. SMC points calculator In general you will be able to claim points if you: have a qualification that is at least a or higher have an occupational registration that requires at least 2 years of training or experience (for example, if you are a registered accountant, electrician, or plumber), or are paid at least 1.5 times the SMC wage threshold. Tip You can find out more about the SMC wage threshold in the ‘Common SMC terms’ section of this page. Examples You may be able to claim 6 points in different ways: if you have a bachelor's degree and some New Zealand skilled work experience if you have eligible occupational registration and some New Zealand work experience if you are paid at least 1.5 times the SMC wage threshold and have some New Zealand skilled work experience if you have a New Zealand master's degree that you studied full-time for 30 weeks. You cannot mix points from different skill categories. For example, you cannot combine qualification points with income points. You can only combine 1 skill category with skilled work experience in New Zealand. If you are using New Zealand skilled work experience to claim points, it will need to have been paid at the SMC wage threshold, or higher if you are also claiming points from income. Skilled Work Experience pathway The Skilled Work Experience pathway is for people who have built their skills through work experience. This pathway may suit you if you work in a skilled role and have several years of experience in that type of work. To use this pathway, you must have: a job or job offer in an skill level 1 to 3 occupation pay of at least 1.1 times the SMC wage threshold at least 3 years of relevant work experience, and an additional 2 years of skilled work experience in New Zealand. Some jobs have extra rules. If your job is on the Amber List, you must meet higher requirements. If your job is on the Red List, you cannot use this pathway. Red and Amber list occupations You cannot count self-employment as relevant work experience for this pathway. Tip You can find out what the SMC wage threshold, relevant work experience and skilled work experience mean in the ‘Common SMC terms’ section of this page. Trades and Technician pathway The Trades and Technician pathway is for people in eligible trades and technician occupations. This pathway may suit you if you work in a practical or technical role and have both a relevant qualification and work experience. Eligible roles may include some jobs in areas like: construction engineering manufacturing automotive trades technical support roles. Eligible occupations for the SMC Trades and Technician pathway To use this pathway, you must have: a job in an eligible trades or technician occupation pay of at least the SMC wage threshold a relevant qualification at , or comparable to level 4 or higher at least 2.5 years of relevant post-qualification work experience, and an additional 1.5 years of post-qualification skilled work experience in New Zealand. You cannot count self-employment as relevant work experience for this pathway. Tip You can find out what the SMC wage threshold, relevant work experience and skilled work experience mean in the ‘Common SMC terms’ section of this page. How to know which pathway may fit you The best SMC pathway for you will depend on what skills you have. If you have a degree or occupational registration The Points-based pathway may be the best place to start. Check how many points your qualification or registration is worth, then check whether you need New Zealand skilled work experience to reach 6 points. If you are highly paid The Points-based pathway may be the best place to start. Your income may give you enough points to apply, or it may give you some points that you can combine with skilled work experience in New Zealand. If you have strong work experience but do not have a degree The Skilled Work Experience pathway may be the best place to start. This pathway is designed for people who can show they have developed their skills through work experience. If you work in a trades or technician role The Trades and Technician pathway may be the best place to start. You need to check whether your occupation is eligible and whether your qualification and work experience meets the requirements. If you are eligible for more than 1 pathway If you have a combination of skills, for example if you have a master’s degree and a lot of work experience you may be eligible for more than 1 SMC pathway. You can find guidance on what to do in this situation, as well as information about other New Zealand residence pathways here: Getting New Zealand residence through your skills Common SMC terms Accredited employer An accredited employer is an employer approved by Immigration New Zealand to hire migrants. For SMC, your skilled job or job offer must be with an accredited employer. ANZSCO (Australian and New Zealand Standard Classification of Occupations) ANZSCO is a system used to classify jobs by skill level. Many SMC requirements depend on whether your job is ANZSCO skill level 1 to 3, or ANZSCO skill level 4 to 5. Find your job's skill level SMC wage threshold The SMC wage threshold is the pay rate used to check whether your job or work experience meets the wage requirements. Some pathways require you to be paid the SMC wage threshold. Others require a higher rate, such as 1.1, 1.2 or 1.5 times the SMC wage threshold. The SMC wage threshold that applies to you will depend on which pathway you are applying for, and when you started gaining skilled work experience in New Zealand (if applicable). You can find which SMC wage threshold applies to you here: The SMC wage threshold Skilled resident points For the SMC Points-based pathway, we use a points system called skilled resident points. You need 6 points to be eligible for an SMC Resident Visa. Expression of interest (EOI) An expression of interest is the first step in applying for the SMC Resident Visa. If your EOI shows you meet the requirements, you may be invited to apply for residence. Relevant work experience Relevant work experience is work experience related to your current job or job offer. Skilled work experience Skilled work experience is work experience related to your current job or job offer, completed in New Zealand, and paid at the required amount. What to do next Check which SMC pathway may fit you. Check the full Skilled Migrant Category Resident Visa requirements. Gather evidence for the pathway you want to apply under. Submit an EOI if you meet the requirements. You can find all details of the SMC Resident Visa requirements, including what evidence you will need to apply on the Skilled Migrant Category Resident Visa page.
