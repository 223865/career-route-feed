As a social enterprise, we care about our customers and our impact. We are committed to ensuring that immigrants, refugees, and international students can learn, work, and thrive whether through our role as a provider of credential evaluations, an organizer of collaborative initiatives, an advocate of policies that recognize global talent, or as a funder committed to economic inclusion.
