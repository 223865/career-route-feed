Yes. Your assessing authority can email your Letter/Certificate of Good Standing to assessment.connect@adc.org.au Do I need to complete an English test as part of my initial assessment application? You do not need to complete an English test for any stage of the ADC dental practitioner assessment process. However, you should note that the written and practical examinations are delivered in English and your ability to communicate effectively will be assessed as part of your practical examination. Successful candidates applying for registration must meet the Dental Board of Australia English language skills registration standard.
