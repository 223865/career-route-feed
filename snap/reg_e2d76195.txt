In Canada, physiotherapists are licenced by provincial and territorial Regulatory Colleges. While Regulatory Colleges have many similar requirements, each jurisdiction may have variations or unique conditions for licensure. The Canadian Alliance of Physiotherapy Regulators (CAPR) is a separate organization that works with all Regulatory Colleges. We provide important credentialling and examination functions which are prerequisite to licencing. For more information, contact the Regulatory Colleges in the province or territory you plan on practising in.
